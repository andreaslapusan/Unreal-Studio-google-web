Uluwatu sits at the southern tip of Bali, on the Bukit Peninsula (South Kuta district, Badung regency). It is a limestone plateau ringed by cliffs, with white-sand beaches at the foot of small bays and some of the best-known surf breaks in the world. Over the last decade it has grown from a surf destination into an area of luxury hotels, beach clubs and private villas, and it is now one of the most in-demand holiday rental markets on the island.

We build in four Uluwatu micro-areas: Melasti, Pandawa, Balangan and Karma Beach. Each has its own type of guest, price level and access times. This guide covers them one by one and shows our projects in each, including the ones already sold.

Why invest in Uluwatu

  • Its own tourist demand. Uluwatu does not rely on one type of visitor. It draws surfers all year, luxury travellers around the big resorts and beach clubs, and remote workers on long stays.
  • Close to the airport. From Balangan or Pandawa the airport is around half an hour away outside rush hour. For a guest staying a weekend or a week, that matters.
  • High-end offer nearby. Hotels such as The Apurva Kempinski, The Ritz-Carlton and the Hilton, the New Kuta golf course and beach clubs like Savaya, Sundays and Palmilla make Uluwatu an expensive destination, which supports nightly rates for nearby villas.
  • Less crowded than Canggu. The southern Bukit has more space and less building density than the Canggu coast, although it is growing fast.
  • Product that fits the investor. One- to three-bedroom villas and lofts, with private or shared pools, matched to the guests the area attracts.

Melasti

Melasti is in Ungasan, on the south-east coast of the Bukit. The road down to the beach is cut into the cliff, and the bay, with calm turquoise water, is one of the most photographed on the island. Around it are beach clubs, restaurants and wedding venues. Guests here come for a swimming beach, not just surf.

Pandawa

Pandawa (Kutuh village) is on the south coast of the Bukit, between Melasti and Nusa Dua. Its beach, reached through rock walls carved with statues, is wide with calm water. Some of the largest resorts in south Bali are a few minutes away. It is the micro-area with the most room to grow: there is still land, and new construction is only now arriving.

Balangan

Balangan is on the west side of the Bukit, next to the New Kuta golf course and close to Dreamland and Bingin. Balangan beach is long, with a left-hand wave and a more laid-back surf atmosphere than Melasti. It is one of the best-connected micro-areas, both to the airport and to the Bukit's restaurants. It is also where we have built the most.

Karma Beach

Karma Beach is at the southern end of Ungasan, below the Karma Kandara cliffs, between Melasti and Nyang Nyang. This is the area of open ocean views and high-end beach clubs (Sundays and Palmilla are close by, Savaya a few minutes away).

Bingin, Padang Padang and Nyang Nyang

We have no projects in these areas, but they are worth knowing because they set the price ceiling of the Bukit. Bingin and Padang Padang are the best-known surf bays, with cliffside villas and strong demand. Nyang Nyang is a long, lightly built beach reached on foot. Sea-view land here is among the most expensive in south Bali, and its proximity supports values in Balangan and Karma Beach.

How payment works

Bank financing is not available in Bali for a foreign buyer in this kind of purchase. Off-plan purchases are paid in milestones during construction: a reservation and several payments tied to stages (start, structure, roof, finishes, handover). Each payment follows the real progress of the build, documented with reports and photos. That is how all our Uluwatu projects work.

Rentals: seasons and guests

  • High season: July-August and Christmas-New Year. The dry season coincides with the best waves on the Bukit's west coast.
  • Shoulder season: April-June and September-October, with good occupancy from couples and long stays.
  • Low season: February-March (rainy season), when rates drop.
  • What rents best: one- and two-bedroom villas with private pools for couples, and three-bedroom villas for families and groups.

What to check before buying in Uluwatu

  1. Land use. The plot must be in a zone that allows tourist use if you plan nightly rentals. Ask for the zoning document.
  2. Building permit. The villa should have a building permit (PBG), or an application already filed.
  3. Leasehold years and extension. Check how many years remain from handover, not from signing, and on what terms it can be extended. More in leasehold vs freehold in Indonesia.
  4. Water and access. The Bukit is a dry limestone plateau: ask how water is supplied (mains, well or tank) and what car access is like.
  5. Who builds it. Visit the site or ask for recent reports with photos. A developer that designs and builds is accountable for the whole process.
  6. Notary. The contract is signed before a notary. What the notary does: the PPAT notary in a Bali purchase.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Uluwatu from Bali airport?

Between 15 and 25 km depending on the micro-area. Outside rush hour it takes about 30 minutes to Balangan or Pandawa and 40-60 minutes to Melasti, Karma Beach or Uluwatu Temple.

Can a foreigner buy a villa in Uluwatu?

Yes, usually through a leasehold: a right of use for a set number of years, registered before a notary and extendable. Some projects, such as Amante Villas, are also offered as freehold; the exact structure is reviewed with the notary.

Which part of Uluwatu is best for investment?

It depends on your goal. Balangan is the best connected and has a surf feel; Melasti attracts guests who want a swimming beach; Karma Beach sells ocean views; Pandawa has the most room to grow because new building is only now arriving.

Is there a mortgage or financing for buying in Uluwatu?

No. Bank financing is not available in Bali for a foreign buyer in this kind of purchase. Off-plan property is paid in milestones during construction, tied to real progress.

What return does a villa in Uluwatu make?

It depends on the micro-area, nightly rate, occupancy and management. Any figure is a projection and is not guaranteed. We explain how it is calculated for each project before you reserve.

Can I see the construction without travelling to Bali?

Yes. We run video calls from the site and send photo reports during construction. You can book a call on the website.

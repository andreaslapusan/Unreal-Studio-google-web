Tabanan is the regency that begins where Canggu ends. West of Cemagi the coast continues through Kedungu, Nyanyi, Tanah Lot and Pasut, with rice fields running to the sea, black-sand beaches and far less construction. Inland are the Jatiluwih rice terraces, protected by UNESCO as part of the cultural landscape of the subak irrigation system. It is the greenest, least built-up Bali within reach of the south-west coast.

For an investor, Tabanan is a long-term play: land is cheaper than in Canggu, tourist demand grows as Canggu fills up, and the guests who come are looking for nature, wellness and quiet.

Why Tabanan

  • It is Canggu's natural extension. The market is moving west: first Pererenan, then Seseh and Cemagi, and now Kedungu and the Tabanan coast.
  • Cheaper land. Land costs a fraction of Canggu prices, which means larger plots and more spacious villas for the same budget.
  • Projects that draw visitors. Nuanu, a creative city with art, wellness and dining spaces next to Nyanyi beach, has brought in visitors who used to pass Tabanan by.
  • A different guest. People who choose Tabanan want rice fields, uncrowded surf, yoga retreats and time off-grid. It is a niche with good rates for well-designed villas.
  • Better access on the way. A toll road between Gilimanuk and Mengwi is planned, which will improve the link between the Tabanan coast and the south of the island.

Kedungu and Nyanyi

Kedungu is the first stretch of Tabanan coast west of Cemagi. It has a wave well known among surfers, cafés, surf schools and villas among rice fields. Nyanyi, right next to it, is home to Nuanu. It is the part of Tabanan with the most services and the closest to Canggu.

Pasut and Tibubiu

Further west, in the village of Tibubiu, is Pasut beach: black sand, a river mouth, fishing boats and very little construction. It is the quietest area we work in, and land here allows large villas with open views over the rice fields.

Who it suits

  • It suits you if you think long term, want more villa and more land for your money, and like the idea of a nature and wellness guest.
  • It suits you less if you want high-turnover rentals with nightlife and restaurants on foot, or need guests to arrive quickly from the airport. For that, look at Uluwatu or Canggu.

Things to keep in mind

  1. Fewer services on foot. There are fewer restaurants and shops than in Canggu. The villa has to stand on its own: design, pool, views, a good kitchen.
  2. Land use is key. Much of Tabanan is protected rice field. For nightly rentals the plot must be in a zone that allows tourist use. Ask for the zoning document.
  3. Further from the airport. Between an hour and a quarter and two hours. Stays tend to be longer to make the trip worthwhile.
  4. The market grows more slowly. Tabanan rewards the patient investor more than someone after a quick return. Returns depend on the nightly rate, occupancy and management, and any figure is a projection, not an assured result.
  5. Tenure. Check with the notary whether you are buying leasehold or freehold, and on what terms. More in leasehold vs freehold in Indonesia.

How payment works

Bank financing is not available in Bali for a foreign buyer in this kind of purchase. New builds are paid in milestones during construction, with each payment tied to a stage and documented with reports and photos.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Tabanan?

It is the regency west of Badung, on Bali's south-west coast. Its coastline starts at Kedungu, just past Cemagi, and runs on to Tanah Lot, Pasut and Balian.

Why invest in Tabanan instead of Canggu?

Land is cheaper, there is more space and less rental competition. In exchange there are fewer services on foot and the market grows more slowly: it suits a long-term investor.

How far is Tabanan from the airport?

35 to 50 km depending on the area: from an hour and a quarter to two hours by car, depending on traffic.

What is Nuanu?

A creative city next to Nyanyi beach, with art, wellness and dining spaces. It has brought visitors to the Tabanan coast who used to pass the area by.

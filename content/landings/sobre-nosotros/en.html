Unreal Studio Bali is a property developer and architecture studio that designs, builds and markets its own projects in Bali: villas, lofts and apartments designed for holiday rental. Most of our buyers are investors from Spain and Europe, served by a team in Bali and an office in Madrid. In our developments, each project starts in our studio, is built by local contractors under our supervision, and we stay with the buyer until the villa is rented.

Who we are

Unreal Studio Bali was founded by Andreas Lapusan, who today runs the studio and oversees the design and construction of every project.

The team works from two places:

  • Bali: architecture, site management, purchasing, photography and follow-up on every project, with a local team that knows the land, suppliers and procedures.
  • Madrid: looking after buyers in Spain, presentations and reservations, in Spanish and in the client's time zone.

What we do

  • We design every project in our studio with rentals in mind: size, pool, orientation, materials that stand up to a tropical climate and easy maintenance.
  • We build with trusted local contractors, with our own supervision on site.
  • We market each project unit by unit, with Spanish-speaking support for buyers from Madrid and Bali.
  • We stay involved after handover: furnishing if the buyer wants it, getting the villa ready for rental and connecting the owner with a management company.

We work in three areas of Bali: Uluwatu (Melasti, Pandawa, Balangan and Karma Beach), Canggu and Pererenan and Tabanan.

How we work: from design to management

1. Land and design

Before drawing anything we check the land: that the zone allows tourist use, access, water and what can be built. We then design the whole project in our studio, including the number of units, layouts and finishes. Buyers see plans, renders and the site plan before reserving.

2. Reservation and contract

The purchase is formalised in a contract before a notary. In most projects the buyer acquires a leasehold: a right of use for a set number of years, registered before a notary and extendable. Some projects are also offered as freehold. We explain the difference in our leasehold and freehold guide.

3. Construction and milestone payments

Bank financing is not available in Bali for a foreign buyer in this kind of purchase, so we do not offer it. Construction is paid in milestones: a reservation and several payments tied to construction stages. Each payment matches progress that can be checked.

4. Construction reports

During construction we send reports with photos and video of progress. Every buyer has access to a private portal with their documents, payment schedule and project reports. If you want to see the site live, we do a video call from the plot, and buyers who travel to Bali can visit.

5. Handover

We hand over the villa finished, with all fixtures installed: doors and windows, floors, bathrooms, kitchen, built-in wardrobes and pool. Full furnishing for rental is an optional package agreed at the end of construction.

6. Rental management

We work with one or two trusted management companies in each area and negotiate terms for our buyers. It is optional: owners can choose another company or manage the villa themselves, and we help them set it up either way. How it works from abroad: managing a Bali villa remotely.

What we do not do

  • We do not offer financing or mortgages. Payment is in milestones during construction.
  • We do not guarantee returns. The rental figures we give are projections based on area, property type and market, and are presented as such.
  • We are not tax advisers. We share how other investors handle it, but each buyer's tax position should be reviewed by an adviser.
  • We do not make our management compulsory. The owner chooses.

## 2. What data we process

- **Contact and identification data:** first name and surname, email address, telephone and WhatsApp number, country, language, postal address.
- **Commercial interest data:** projects consulted, budget, preferences, messages, notes of calls and meetings, origin of the contact (campaign, advertisement, agency, referral).
- **Contractual and client data:** identity document or passport, nationality, date of birth, marital status and matrimonial property regime where necessary, data of co-owners, companies or vehicles through which the contract is entered into, bank and payment details, receipts, payment schedule, unit acquired, communications and contract documentation.
- **Fraud and money laundering prevention data:** source of funds, beneficial ownership and identity checks, where appropriate.
- **Portal access data:** username, encrypted credentials, access logs, preferred language.
- **Partner and agency data:** professional contact details, billing details, activity on the agency portal, leads provided and commissions.
- **Browsing data:** IP address, device and cookie identifiers, browser, pages visited, campaign parameters (UTM) and conversion events (see section 10).
- **Recordings:** with prior notice, audio or video recordings of meetings and calls, and their transcripts and summaries (section 6).
- **Submitted content:** documents, photographs and property data sent through forms or listing links.

We do not request special categories of data (health, ideology, etc.). Please do not provide them. If the User provides data of third parties (co-owners, family members, business partners), they warrant that they have informed them and have their authorisation.

## 6. Recording of meetings and calls

We may record in-person meetings, video calls and sales or follow-up calls, and generate transcripts and summaries using tools that may use artificial intelligence. Notice will always be given before or at the start of the meeting. Recordings are used to document what was discussed, prepare documentation and improve the service; they are kept confidential and are not published. The data subject may object to the recording at the start of the meeting. What is said in a meeting does not modify the contracts (see the Terms and Conditions).

Due to the nature of our activity (projects in Indonesia, clients in Europe and global suppliers), data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area:

- **Indonesia:** to the Unreal Entities and to Indonesian notaries, authorities and suppliers, where necessary for the performance of the contract requested by the data subject or of pre-contractual measures (art. 49.1.b and c GDPR (RGPD)) or under standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (art. 46 GDPR (RGPD)).
- **United States and other countries:** to providers such as Google, Meta, Cloudflare, Resend or HighLevel, which adhere to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where certified, or apply standard contractual clauses and supplementary measures.

Transfers of data from Indonesia are carried out in accordance with the requirements of article 56 of the PDP Law. The data subject may request information on the safeguards applied by writing to the contact address.

## 8. Rights of data subjects

The data subject may at any time exercise their rights of **access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing, portability** and **not to be subject to automated individual decision-making**, as well as **withdraw their consent** without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. Under the PDP Law, they may also request information on the identity of the controller and the purpose of the processing, complete or update their data, suspend or restrict the processing and lodge claims for infringements in accordance with that law.

**How to exercise them:** by writing to hello@unrealstudiobali.com, with the subject "Data protection", indicating the right exercised and providing, if necessary to verify their identity, a copy of an identity document. We will respond within one month, extendable in the cases provided for by law (or within the period established by the PDP Law where applicable). To unsubscribe from commercial communications, it is sufficient to use the link included in each communication or to reply requesting it.

These rights may be restricted where permitted by law, for example, where we must retain data due to a legal obligation or for the defence of claims.

**Complaint to the supervisory authority:** if you consider that we have not properly addressed your rights, you may lodge a complaint with the **Agencia Española de Protección de Datos** (www.aepd.es, C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid), with the authority of your EU State of residence or, where applicable, with the competent authority in Indonesia. We would be grateful if you would contact us first to try to resolve the matter.

## 9. Retention periods

- **Prospective clients who do not enter into a contract:** for as long as there is mutual interest and, at most, two years from the last contact, unless they have previously objected or withdrawn consent.
- **Clients:** for the duration of the contractual relationship (including the warranty period and, where applicable, the term of the right acquired) and, thereafter, for the limitation periods of legal actions and of the applicable commercial, tax and accounting obligations (as a general rule, up to six years in Spain and the equivalent periods in Indonesia).
- **Anti-money laundering data:** ten years, where Law 10/2010 applies.
- **Meeting recordings:** for as long as they are useful for the purpose and, at most, for the duration of the relationship with the data subject and the applicable limitation periods.
- **Commercial communications:** until the data subject objects or withdraws their consent.
- **Cookies:** according to the period indicated in section 10.

Once those periods have elapsed, the data will be erased or anonymised, or blocked where required by law.

## 11. Security and breaches

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk (encryption in transit, access and credential control, role-based permissions, backups and confidentiality of staff and partners). No system is infallible; the User must safeguard their credentials and notify us of any incident. In the event of a security breach, we will notify the supervisory authority and, where appropriate, the persons affected within the legal time limits (72 hours under the GDPR (RGPD) and 3 x 24 hours under the PDP Law, where applicable).

**Anti-fraud notice:** we will never ask you for passwords by email or message, nor will we notify you of a change of bank account by a simple email. If in any doubt, contact us through the official channels.

## 12. Minors
